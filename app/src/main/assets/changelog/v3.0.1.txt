FLOW CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 3.0.1
DATE: 2026-10-27
STATUS: pre-release

HIGHLIGHTS
- Android TV is redesigned on Material 3 Expressive, using the same pages as the phone app
- A navigation rail built from your own tabs, with a Now playing item while music plays
- The music and video players match the phone's, with the expressive play button and controls
- Search uses your TV's own keyboard and voice search
- Downloads now work on TV

ANDROID TV: BROWSING
- Home, Subscriptions, Library, playlists, history and channels are the phone's pages, laid out for the big screen
- Feeds show three cards across so titles and channel names fit
- Picking a tab on the rail again takes you back to its first page, and picking it once more scrolls up and refreshes
- Each card is one focus stop, and the Menu key or a long press opens its quick actions
- The focused card lifts with a focus ring, and pages open with focus on their content instead of the rail
- Back arrows, channel tabs, shelf headers and the buttons under artist and album headers are all reachable with the remote
- On a channel, the header folds away while you browse its videos

ANDROID TV: PLAYERS
- The video player uses the phone's seek bar, transport, sheets, skip button and autoplay countdown
- Player buttons for captions, save, comments, info and settings sit in one connected row
- Related videos show three large cards across with their full details
- The music player is the phone's wide player, and the mini player floats at the bottom like on the phone
- Every mini player button can be reached: open, previous, play or pause, next and close
- Pages leave room at the bottom so the mini player never covers their last items

ANDROID TV: SEARCH, MUSIC AND SETTINGS
- Search opens the TV's own keyboard, has a voice search button and a Videos or Music switch
- Music home, artists, albums, playlists and moods are the phone's pages
- Settings are the phone's settings, plus a page explaining the remote's buttons
- Downloads appear in Library with their own page, and Downloads settings are available on TV
- Sync works on TV as the host device, since TVs have no camera to scan a code

PLAYBACK AND NOTIFICATIONS
- The music media card shows shuffle and repeat, and like and close stay on older Android, Auto and Wear
- Shuffle from the media card, a headset, a watch or the car now shuffles the queue you see in the app
- The video notification has repeat, and shuffle while a queue is playing
- DeArrow shows a frame from the video when nobody has submitted a thumbnail, as DeArrow itself does, with a switch to turn that off
- DeArrow titles also appear on Shorts cards
- Lyrics add lrc.red and Unison as sources, find word-by-word timing more often, and no longer give live or remixed songs the studio version's timing
- Choose what swiping a song in Up next does on each side, including removing it, with Undo
- Move any saved playlist between Music and Videos from its menu
- A Google Takeout import puts playlists of music in Music

FIXES
- Fix a paused video not playing again after the screen was off for a while
- Fix a video stopping for good after a decoder error; it now recovers once, then offers Retry
- Fix a short glitch at the start of the next song when one song plays into the next
- Fix DeArrow looking a video up again every time its card scrolled back into view
- Fix the music player's playback buttons not responding to a keyboard, a remote or TalkBack
- Fix the TV seek bar redrawing every frame while paused
- Fix Open Collective not opening from About on TV
- Fix paused music keeping the phone awake for hours when the equalizer is off
- Fix Liked music showing oldest first after restoring a backup
- Fix subscription groups jumping back while you drag them into a new order
