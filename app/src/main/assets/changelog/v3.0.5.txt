FLOW CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 3.0.5
DATE: 2026-11-16
STATUS: stable

HIGHLIGHTS
- Profiles: up to five people can each have their own feed, history, subscriptions and settings in one app
- Linked devices: keep your phones, tablets, TVs and Flow Desktop in sync over your Wi-Fi, your own Flow server or a WebDAV folder, end to end encrypted
- Play on: move what you're watching or listening to onto another device, or control it from here
- Android TV is redesigned on Material 3 Expressive, using the phone's own pages and players
- Music crossfade, music casting, a customizable mini player and new player backgrounds
- Lyrics: a new Cadence animation, faster lookups with more sources, lyrics on the lock screen and shareable lyric cards
- Ambient and Immersive glow, 360° videos, related videos in fullscreen and a player you can scroll away
- A Downloads tab, a new download sheet with audio track choice, and Buffer ahead on disk
- App lock, a daily watch time limit, and switches to hide or label AI-generated content

BEHAVIOR CHANGES
- Stable Voice is replaced by Normalize volume, which is off by default, so videos play at their full volume (#1335, #1641)
- Audio offload is now off by default, since it made songs play silently on some phones. Turn it on in the music player's Audio settings (#1417)
- Picture-in-picture starts by itself when you leave a playing video, and closing its window pauses the video (#1349)
- SponsorBlock's Ignore action is now called Show in seek bar (#1673)
- Downloads no longer ask for access to all files (#1255)
- Vibration in Appearance now turns vibration on or off across the whole app
- Phones in landscape show the side navigation rail instead of the bottom bar (#1845)

PROFILES
- Up to five people each get their own feed, history, subscriptions, settings and recommendations (#372, #1039)
- Switch profiles from the profile button, now on every main tab, or on TV from the rail and a Who's watching? screen
- Settings can move into the profile button's menu

LINKED DEVICES
- Link phones, tablets, TVs and Flow Desktop into one group that stays in sync, end to end encrypted (#1075)
- Keep the group on your own Flow sync server, in a WebDAV folder such as Nextcloud, or on your Wi-Fi network with no server at all
- Devices on the same Wi-Fi sync directly when they meet, and changes arrive within seconds while both are open
- A TV or other device without a camera joins by showing a code a linked phone scans, or by typing the server address
- Choose which profiles sync and what each device syncs, and turn off Sync automatically to sync only when you tap Sync now
- Recovery words bring the group back if every device is lost, and can be replaced from any device
- Removing a device signs it out for good and lists the devices it added
- Sync devices is redesigned: a status card with Sync now, your devices first, and setup that starts with Create a group or Join a group
- Restoring a backup while linked makes this device leave the group instead of deleting data on your other devices

PLAY ON
- Move what plays to another linked device, or control it from here, with the devices button in the players and the mini player (#1412)
- While you control another device, everything you play goes there, and the notification, lock screen, headset and car control it
- The Play on sheet shows what plays where, with artwork, controls and volume, and your devices right below
- When another device is playing and nothing plays here, the mini player shows it with Play here
- The first time a device asks to play here you choose Allow always, Allow once or Decline
- Android TV receives handoffs, and its rail shows which device you are playing on

ANDROID TV
- Home, Subscriptions, Library, Music, Settings and channels are the phone's pages, laid out for the big screen with a rail built from your own tabs
- The video and music players match the phone's, with the expressive play button and a floating mini player
- Search uses the TV's own keyboard and voice search, with a Videos or Music switch
- Downloads and Downloads settings work on TV, and a new page explains the remote's buttons
- Every card, tab, button and player control is reachable with the remote, and the Menu key or holding OK opens quick actions
- Reorder tabs, playlists, groups, lyrics providers and the queue with Move up and Move down
- A Now playing item sits in the rail while music plays, and going back from a video returns to the card you played
- Open a channel from the video player, and links in channels and descriptions open inside Flow
- Seeking with the remote shows only the progress bar, and Up next sits on its own panel (#998)

MUSIC PLAYER
- Crossfade between songs for up to 12 seconds, with fade curves and a choice of when it runs; album tracks stay gapless (#1675)
- Next and Previous keep the old song playing until the next one starts, so the gap between songs is mostly gone (#1761, #2076)
- Cast music to your TV or speaker, including songs on your phone and downloads (#1380)
- Five new backgrounds: Solid Color, Cover Colors, Moving Colors, Cover Shapes and True Black
- Previous and next sit against play and pause as a split button, which you can turn off
- Select several songs in the queue to play next, add to a playlist, download or remove (#2084)
- Swipe on the left side for speed and on the right for volume, and speed now goes up to 4x (#1403, #1346)
- Podcasts and long tracks can resume where you stopped, and a Resume from headphones setting (#1346, #1450)
- An optional lock screen player with the cover and controls (#1859)
- Tapping the artist lets you pick any credited artist (#1638)
- Animated artwork shows the whole picture, keeps moving while paused and is found for more songs (#1702, #1711, #1764)
- Seeking in a song no longer stops to load it again (#2167)

MUSIC MINI PLAYER
- Pick up to three buttons from thirteen, and choose what tapping the cover or holding the bar does
- The cover morphs through Material shapes as music plays, with the progress ring following its outline
- Show progress as a fill, an edge line, or a straight or wavy line, and pick its background
- It uses much less battery while it plays

LYRICS
- New Cadence animation, where letters rise as they are sung and long notes swell. Classic and Calm are still there
- YouTube Music's own timed lyrics come first, and lrc.red, Unison, NetEase and AMLL join the sources (#1782, #1817)
- Lyrics appear much faster, the next song's lyrics load ahead, and songs without lyrics are remembered (#1782)
- Lyrics match the right song and version more often, a source you pick stays picked, and your sync offset stays with each song (#1782)
- Lyrics on the lock screen, in the notification and in the mini player (#1742, #1972)
- Share lyrics as an image in Classic, Poster or Shape layouts and square or story sizes (#1940, #2005)
- Colorful and Moving lyrics backgrounds, a switch for the blur, and lyrics over the cover in Immersive (#1739, #1453, #1278)
- Romanization only, instrumental dots in line-synced lyrics, and (×2) on repeated lines (#1803, #1811)
- Lyrics can fit music videos by skipping parts with no music, using SponsorBlock (#1742)
- Load lyrics only when opened, and see which servers each provider contacts (#1860)
